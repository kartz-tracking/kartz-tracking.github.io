Leaving the test group

For anyone using the draft version of TW Data Extractor.

Draft testers run a version of the add-on that has not been reviewed by Google yet. Nobody is enrolled without being asked, and you can stop at any time — you do not need a reason and nothing is lost by leaving.

To stop testing

  1. Email jk06nm04@gmail.com from the address you were added with, saying you would like to be removed. You will be taken off the list.
  2. If you want the add-on gone from your account as well, remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What happens then

Nothing is deleted from your spreadsheets — the rows the add-on wrote are ordinary rows and stay exactly where they are. The add-on holds no data of yours anywhere else; see the privacy policy.

Once the add-on has been reviewed and published, the draft group is no longer needed and everyone uses the reviewed version instead.
